The marks exist so that people can tell the official ezCHAIN from a copy. In software that touches
money, a copy that borrows the name can cost someone their funds.

## The official ezCHAIN

The official source is `github.com/0xJ1nn/ezchain`, and official releases are the tagged releases in
that repository. Anything else is not the official ezCHAIN, whatever it is called.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your project is compatible with ezCHAIN, works with ezCHAIN, or uses the ezCHAIN SDK.
- Describe a fork accurately, for example "based on ezCHAIN", in its README or about text.
- Name ezCHAIN in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks and comparisons, as long as you do not suggest
  the project endorses you.
- Describe a strategy plugin as "for ezCHAIN".

## You may not, without written permission

- Publish a fork, a build or a hosted service under the name ezCHAIN, or under a name or logo that
  could be confused with it (for example "ezCHAIN Pro" or "ezchain-cloud").
- Use the marks in a product name, company name, domain name, social account, app-store listing,
  token name or token ticker.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests the project endorses, audits or guarantees your product,
  strategy, signals or returns.
- Change the logo or combine it with another mark.

## Forks

Forking is welcome under the code licences. A fork that is distributed or offered as a service must
use its own name and logo, keep the [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) file, and remove the ezCHAIN logo from its
interface, icons and store listings.

## Asking for permission or reporting misuse

Write to the contact address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. To report a copy that pretends to
be ezCHAIN, include where it appears and why it could mislead people.
