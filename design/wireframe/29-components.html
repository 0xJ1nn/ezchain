RIDING · NO MAX HOLD
Z1 · identity + headline
Avatar, name, symbol // venue // chain, position id (detail only). PnL % is the headline; USD and native amount sit under it. Equity value belongs here, never hidden in a tooltip.
Z2 · price + provenance
Live mark first, then freshness/source, then mode, chain, age, hold. Entry price is shown on the chart and full page; the tile may omit it only if it stays in the tooltip.
Z3 · chart with levels
Entry, hard stop, trail, arm/bank/rungs. A level appears only after 75% of the distance is covered (no look-ahead). Peak dot and label share one source. Stale tail is dashed.
Z4 · market strip
MCAP · LIQ · HOLDERS · 1H · M5 · BSR. Each cell has source + age; live overrides entry snapshot; missing shows —.
Z5 · money split
IN · BANKED · REALIZED · UNREAL, all from the ledger. Banked is cash recovered, not profit.
Z6 · ladder + next
BAG %, state chip, four ticks (ARM/BANK/RUNG/FINAL) and the next objective. RIDING removes max-hold.
Z7 · provenance
Mark label + age, exec/gate status, rule name, net PnL and fees. Every badge has a tooltip with the exact value and source.
Z8 · budget + actions
Shrinking hold budget or RIDING. SELL NOW single click; WRITE OFF two-step; ⋯ for note/export/explorer.
Z9 · note
One line, full text on hover. Notes are operator memory, not trading logic.

Chart frame

required elements
Entry Hard stop Trail Arm / bank Stale tail
Axes carry values, not just numbers. Y labels are prices in the position's quote. X covers the whole hold window; the live dot sits at the right edge. Hover gives time, price, value and PnL. Any level shown must be earned (75% rule).
