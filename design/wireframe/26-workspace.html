Approval policy

what needs a second human

Any key with trade scope, including read-only keys upgraded later.
Dual approval for withdrawals
Cold-watch transfers excluded; they are watch-only by definition.
Trader may install strategies
Install requires owner review of manifest permissions.
A denied approval is final for that intent. Changing the policy does not retroactively approve queued orders.
Live mode itself is enabled in Risk; this policy only gates individual orders.
