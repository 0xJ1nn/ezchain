Rule semantics

• Trigger keys are idempotent: (automation, position, level, day).
• Conditions are evaluated on one snapshot; actions see that snapshot, not a newer one.
• An action that would breach a policy is rejected, not clamped, unless the field is marked clampable.

What this cannot do

• Hold keys or sign transactions.
• Exceed wallet or strategy limits.
• Bypass the paper gate for live mode.
• Retry indefinitely or “keep trying until it fills”.
