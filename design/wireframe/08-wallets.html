Multi-wallet rules

• A position belongs to exactly one wallet; it cannot be split across wallets.
• Sweeps move assets, not positions. A sweep is a transfer intent with its own ledger entries.
• Hardware wallets can hold positions; execution pauses for device approval.
• Watch-only wallets can be selected for signals and backtests, never for execution.
• Exchange balances are read-only until a trade-scope key is explicitly granted.
