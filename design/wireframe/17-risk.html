What the kill switch does not do

• It does not sell your positions. Exits stay available, entries do not.
• It does not reverse or cancel an already-signed on-chain transaction.
• It does not revoke ERC-20 / SPL approvals; a compromised key can still move approved funds.
• It does not pause the chain, the venue or a strategy's own timers.
• It does not reduce drawdown that already happened; it caps the next decision.
Use Security for approvals and key custody, not this switch.
