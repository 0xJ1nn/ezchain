Reading the wireframe

Solid borders
Committed structure: this element and its order are part of the product.
Dashed borders
Placeholder or optional container; visual treatment is open.
Monospace numbers
All data values. Tabular figures are required in the visual pass.
Colour
Only encodes state (profit, loss, warning, error). Final palette: TBD.
