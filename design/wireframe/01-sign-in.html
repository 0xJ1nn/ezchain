Security facts

before you type anything
  • local Keys never leave this machineSigning happens in the local vault process. No key material, seed or signature request is transmitted to ezCHAIN or any third party.
  • vault Encrypted at restArgon2id + XChaCha20-Poly1305. Auto-lock after 15 minutes idle; 12m elapsed on the current unlock.
  • passkey Phishing-resistant by defaultPasskeys are scoped to this origin. An authenticator code is accepted only as fallback.
  • audit Every sign-in is recordedDevice, IP, method and result are appended to the local audit log; 9,412 entries retained.
No cloud account. No telemetry. No recovery by the maintainers.
