Definitions

• Net PnL = realized cash − fees, in USD at fill time.
• Win rate counts closed positions only; DCA entries without an exit are excluded.
• Profit factor = gross wins / gross losses, before unrealized.
• R uses the stop recorded in the position's exit plan v1.

Exclusions

• 60 imported paper trades with no exit quote are not in any metric.
• Write-offs are shown as realized losses with reason WRITE_OFF.
• Unsellable positions stay unrealized, never marked to zero.
