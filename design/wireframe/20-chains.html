Tier legend

verification, not ambition
T1contract tests vs live mainnet
Swaps, balances, transfers, receipts, reorg handling. Used in paper and live.
T2read + transfer verified
Swaps via one aggregator, labelled beta, capped at $75/order.
T3read-only
Balances and positions only. Trade is disabled with CHAIN_READ_ONLY, not hidden.
Honesty rule. The UI reads this matrix. T2 says “beta” next to execution; T3 disables trading and shows the reason. No chain silently looks equal to another.
